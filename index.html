About Me

I work as a wearable robotics software engineer at HUROTICS. I earned my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Chung-Ang University, where I conducted research in the Assistive and Rehabilitation Robotics Lab under the supervision of Prof. Giuk Lee. I am particularly interested in human-robot interaction, reinforcement learning in simulation, and robot control.

Previously, I worked as an Equipment Engineer in the Memory Business of Samsung Electronics' Device Solutions (DS) Division, responsible for EUV lithography equipment. I also received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Chung-Ang University.
